How to Operate NETRA Rover

1. Driving the Rover

Press W, A, S, D or the Arrow Keys on your keyboard, or click the on-screen Drive Pad buttons. Hold to move, release to coast, or press Spacebar to brake instantly.

2. Point-and-Click Map Navigation

Click anywhere on the Tactical Mission Map to drop a destination marker. NETRA's autonomous navigation will calculate the path and steer directly towards it.

3. Speed Control & Modes

Choose between 🐢 Slow (0.8 m/s for rough rocks), 🚗 Normal (1.5 m/s exploration), or ⚡ Turbo (2.5 m/s). Use Return Base anytime to bring the rover back.

4. 3D Showcase & Multi-Cameras

Click the 3D Showcase button in the header or the 3D Model tab to view and inspect NETRA's realistic rocker-bogie chassis in 360°.
